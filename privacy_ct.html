Privacy Policy

Cat Tracks · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Cat Tracks app (the “Game”) collects, why, and what choices you have. The Game is made by Jasper van den Bergh (“we”, “us” or “our”).

In short: you do not need an account to play, we do not ask for your name or email address, and we do not sell your data.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (solved puzzles, earned paws, hints and best times) and your settings (such as sound on or off) are stored on your own device. This information stays on your device unless you use cloud saves.

2. Google Play Games (Android, optional)

On Android you can sign in with Google Play Games. When you do, the Game receives your Play Games player ID and display name, and your game progress is saved in your Google account using Play Games Saved Games. This lets you continue on another device. The data is stored by Google, not by us. You can sign out at any time and manage your Play Games data in your Google account. See the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Usage statistics (Android)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is used, so we can make it better. It records simple events, such as starting a puzzle, starting the daily puzzle, using a hint, solving a puzzle, and whether signing in with Google worked. Together with these events, Firebase collects standard technical information such as:

  • a random app instance ID (not your name or email address);
  • device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • approximate location (country or region), based on your IP address;
  • when and how long the Game is used.

We do not use this information to identify you personally.

4. Crash reports (Android)

If the Game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report to help us find and fix the problem. A crash report contains technical details such as the error, the state of the app, device model, operating system version and a random installation ID.

5. Advertising

The Game may show ads, including optional video ads you can watch for extra hints. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address and information about your interaction with ads, to show ads and measure their performance. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses information from ads.

6. Windows

The Windows version of the Game does not use analytics, crash reporting, sign-in or ads. Your progress is only stored on your computer.

7. Why we use this information

We use the information described above to:

  • let you play and keep your progress, also across devices;
  • find and fix bugs and crashes;
  • understand which features are used, so we can improve the Game;
  • show ads that help keep the Game free.

Under the GDPR, we rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving the Game for statistics and crash reports, on your consent for personalized ads, and on providing the service you ask for when you use cloud saves.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information is only processed by the service providers named above (Google Play Games, Firebase and AdMob, all provided by Google), or when the law requires us to share it. Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards.

9. How long information is kept

  • Data on your device: until you clear the app data or uninstall the Game.
  • Cloud saves: until you delete them in your Google account.
  • Firebase Analytics: up to 14 months.
  • Crashlytics crash reports: up to 90 days.

10. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages, but it is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not know who you are, the easiest way to delete your data is to sign out of Play Games, delete your saved game in your Google account, and uninstall the Game. You can also contact us, and you have the right to file a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
