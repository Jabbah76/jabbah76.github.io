How to play

  1. Every hot spring gets exactly one capybara, right next to it: above, below, left or right.
  2. The numbers along the edges tell you how many capybaras sit in that row or column.
  3. Capybaras never touch each other, not even diagonally.
  4. Cover squares you know stay empty with grass. Use logic, no guessing needed!
