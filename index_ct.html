How to play

  1. Lay one unbroken trail of yarn from the starting ball to the end of the board.
  2. The numbers along the edges tell you how many squares in that row or column hold yarn.
  3. The trail never crosses itself and never branches.
  4. Mark squares you know are empty with a paw print. Use logic, no guessing needed!
